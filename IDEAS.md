# Silly ideas

Small bits of fun that never get in the way of playing: nothing blocks a tap, slows a move or changes the
game.

## Decided

| Idea | Status |
|---|---|
| Fortune line on the win screen (random, or based on stats and time) | Yes |
| Greeting on New game, based on the time of day and date | Yes |
| Silly achievements and titles in the stats screen | Yes |
| Cards holding up scorecards ("9.5, 9.7, 10") after a fast win | Yes, as a rare easter egg |
| Other win animations (bouncing cards, confetti, Kings bowing, ...) | No |
| Odd card faces, holiday card backs | No |
| Easter eggs you trigger yourself, special deal codes | No |
| No-internet jokes (fake leaderboard, cloud sync) | No |
| Developer jokes (credits, solver node counts) | No |
| Stuck and loss messages, sarcastic and a bit dark | Yes |

## Rules for all of it

- One line of small text, never a popup, never in the middle of a move.
- Picked from the deal number, so friends who share a deal code see the same random fortune. Lines based
  on stats take priority over random ones when they apply.
- Don't show the same line twice in a row.

## Stuck and loss messages

Sarcastic and a bit dark. The darkness is aimed at the cards, the deck and the player's pride, never at
real harm (no self-harm, illness or death of people). One line, shown with the existing notice, never
covering the board.

When they show:
- **Stuck**: `MoveAdvisor` says `Stuck` ("no moves left"). The line goes with the notice that already
  suggests undo.
- **Abandoned**: starting a new game while this one was played but not won (counts as a loss).
- **Restart**: the Restart button.
- Not when the player has simply paused: no "you've been idle" nagging.

## Rare scorecards

After a win that was fast or had few moves, about 1 in 20 times, three cards briefly hold up scores over
the foundations ("9.5", "9.7", "10") and then go back. It's only drawn on top, and it's gone in about
2 seconds or on the first tap.
