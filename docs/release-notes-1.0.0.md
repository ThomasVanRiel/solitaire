The first release of Solitaire: a free, open-source patience game for Android where **every deal is proven
winnable**.

Every deal comes with a stored winning sequence. Before a deal reaches you, that sequence has been replayed
through the game's own rules engine, and it is replayed once more on your phone just before the cards are
dealt. Deals can be very hard, but none of them is impossible.

## Games

- **Klondike**: draw 1 or draw 3, unlimited passes through the stock
- **Spider**: 1, 2 or 4 suits
- **Scorpion II**
- **Yukon**
- **Russian Solitaire**: only about 1 deal in 15 can be won, and the app deals only those
- **FreeCell**

Each game has four difficulty levels, Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert, with 2,500 deals in each:
90,000 deals in total.

## Highlights

- Tap a card to send it to the best place, or drag it; legal targets light up while you drag.
- Unlimited undo, a Hint button, and a message when the position can't be won any more.
- A game finishes itself once the rest of it is a formality.
- Share a deal with a short code such as `S2-17`, so a friend can play exactly the same deal.
- Detailed statistics for each game and difficulty, plus 55 achievements.
- Fortune-cookie lines when you win, greetings when you start a game, and slightly dark quips when you're
  stuck.
- A calm look with cards drawn in code, a dark mode, and support for landscape.
- Your game is saved after every move.

## Privacy

No ads, no analytics, no accounts and no network access: the app has no `INTERNET` permission. Your saved
game and statistics stay on your phone. Android's own backup may include them, so they can move with you
to a new phone.

## Install

Download `solitaire-1.0.0.apk` below and open it on your phone. It needs Android 8.0 or newer, and you may
have to allow installing apps from your browser or file manager. The app is not on any app store. To
update later, install the newer APK over this one: your saved game and statistics are kept.

## Known limitations

- The board can't yet be played with a screen reader such as TalkBack. It reads out a summary of the
  position, but the cards can't be selected.
