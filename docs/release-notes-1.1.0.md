## Solitaire 1.1.0

### Playing
- **Taps keep cards in play.** A tapped card now goes onto a card in another column if it fits there,
  rather than to its foundation, so a tap never sends up a card you might still need. Aces, and cards with
  nowhere else to go, still go to the foundation.
- **Tap again to send it on.** Tap a card you just moved and it goes to the next place it can go instead:
  another column, then the foundation. It still counts as one move. Dragging works as before.
- **Left-handed layout.** Turn on *Stock on the left* in New game to put the stock and waste at the left
  and the foundations at the right. Works in portrait and landscape.

### Tidier screens
- New game shows only what you need to play: the game, the difficulty, its rules and the deal code.
- The Statistics notes are shorter, and the version and source link are centred.

### Fortunes
- The "beat the solver" line is gone; it showed after nearly every win.
- Common lines (records, streaks, Expert wins without undo) no longer crowd out the rest.
