Klondike, Spider and Scorpion II patience (solitaire), where every deal can be won.

Every deal ships with a winning sequence of moves that has been replayed through the game's own rules
engine before it reaches you. Deals can still be very hard: the proof uses knowledge of the face-down
cards, which you can't see, so you may get stuck and need to undo. But there is always a way.

• Klondike with draw 1 or draw 3
• Spider with 1, 2 or 4 suits
• Scorpion II
• Four difficulty levels per game, from Easy to Expert
• Unlimited undo
• Tap a card to send it to the best place, or drag it
• Your game is saved after every move

No ads, no analytics, no accounts, and no internet permission. Open source (GPL-3.0-or-later).
